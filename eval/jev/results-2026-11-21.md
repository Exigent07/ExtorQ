## Notes

- Jev outperformed the deterministic baseline on this dataset (77.8% vs 44.4%).

- One run, one call per case; Jev answers can vary between runs and model versions.
- The baseline only uses severity, asset criticality, evidence confidence, correlation counts and coverage gaps; it cannot see actors, review signals or maintenance context.
- Jev output is advisory: it never changes alert severity or suppresses alerts.
